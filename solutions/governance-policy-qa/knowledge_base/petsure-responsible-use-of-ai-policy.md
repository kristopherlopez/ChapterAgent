## About the Document

This Responsible Use of AI Policy (Policy) applies to PetSure Holdings Pty Ltd (PetSure Holdings) and PetSure (Australia) Pty Ltd (PetSure) (collectively, "the Group" and/or "the Boards" as the context requires). PetSure Holdings is authorised by APRA as a Non-Operating Holding Company (NOHC) and is the parent entity of the Level 2 Insurance Group. PetSure is the APRA-authorised Level 1 general insurance entity. PetSure is wholly owned by PetSure Holdings.

This Responsible Use of AI Policy sets out the Group's approach to the responsible use of AI, including guiding principles, approved use cases, and non-compliance implications. This Policy is a key element of the Group's broader Risk Management Framework (RMF).

The use of Artificial Intelligence (AI), including Generative AI, across the Group's activities may result in both opportunities and risks. If not used responsibly, AI may introduce the potential for adverse financial, operational, regulatory, or reputational outcomes. Risks may occur throughout the lifecycle of AI usage — from prompt design, through output validation, to downstream decision-making — and can lead to financial loss, adverse customer outcomes, poor business decisions, reputational damage or regulatory penalties.

A copy of the Policy is made available on the intranet and changes are communicated to relevant stakeholders following updates.

## Scope

The Policy covers all employees, directors, officers, contractors, consultants, and third parties who design, develop, procure, manage, or use AI systems, tools, or features — including those who act on AI-generated outputs to inform business decisions.

**Includes:**

- Use of both public and organisation-approved AI platforms, as well as specific features and integrations outlined in this Policy.
- Application of AI in business operations, customer interactions, product development, internal support functions, and decision-making.
- Management of AI-related risks throughout the full lifecycle of AI use, from initial enquiry or prompt design through to deployment, monitoring, and retirement.
- Consideration of AI risks relating to General Enquiries, Commercially Sensitive Data, and Personally Identifiable Information (PII), as defined in this Policy.

**Excludes:**

- This Policy does not extend to employees' personal use of AI tools outside of their work responsibilities.
- This Policy does not replace regulatory requirements or other internal policies such as Information Security, Privacy and Model Governance. Instead, it complements them by providing a specific framework for responsible AI usage within the Group.

## Definitions

| Term | Definition |
|------|-----------|
| **General Enquiries** | Questions or prompts that do not include company-sensitive or personal data. |
| **Sensitive Data** | Any information relating to internal strategies, financial data, operations, intellectual property, or confidential business information not publicly available. |
| **Personally Identifiable Information (PII)** | Any data that could identify an individual, including names, contact details, addresses, account numbers, or other unique identifiers. |

## Principles of Responsible AI Use

| Principle | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Transparency** | AI use must be clearly understood, with human oversight and accountability. Where AI contributes to decisions affecting customers, the use of AI should be disclosed where appropriate and outputs should be explainable. |
| **Privacy & Security** | Personal and sensitive data must be protected at all times. AI systems must comply with the Australian Privacy Principles and PetSure's information security requirements. |
| **Fairness & Non-Discrimination** | AI must not be used in ways that cause bias, exclusion, or discrimination. AI systems that influence customer outcomes must be tested for bias and monitored for discriminatory impacts. |
| **Accountability** | Human decision-makers remain responsible for AI-assisted outcomes. The use of AI does not transfer or diminish accountability for decisions — the person who acts on an AI-generated output is accountable for that action. |
| **Human-Centricity** | AI should augment people, preserve dignity, and support sound judgement. AI must not replace human judgement in decisions that materially affect customers without appropriate oversight mechanisms. |
| **Safety & Reliability** | AI systems must perform reliably, minimise risks, and avoid unintended harm. AI systems must be tested, monitored, and subject to appropriate controls commensurate with their risk profile. |

## Prohibited Activities

The following categories of AI use are prohibited at PetSure. The AI Acceptable Use Standard translates these principles into specific operational rules and identifies uses that require explicit approval before proceeding.

| Prohibition | Rationale |
|------------|-----------|
| **Discrimination and bias** — Using AI in ways that discriminate based on protected characteristics, or profiling customers beyond legitimate insurance purposes | Fairness and compliance with anti-discrimination legislation |
| **Circumventing human oversight** — Removing or bypassing human review for decisions that significantly affect customers, or relying solely on AI outputs for regulatory submissions, legal decisions, or Board materials | Accountability and regulatory obligations |
| **Unauthorised data use** — Processing personal or sensitive data without appropriate safeguards and authorisation, using customer data to train external models without consent and legal review, or entering confidential information into public AI tools | Privacy, data protection, and information security |
| **Deceptive or misleading content** — Generating misleading or deceptive content for customers, partners, or regulators, or creating synthetic records or fabricated evidence | Integrity, conduct obligations, and customer trust |

## Compliance

A breach of this Policy by an employee may result in disciplinary action, which may include termination of employment in cases of a serious breach of this Policy. Any instances of non-compliance should be treated in accordance with the Incident Management Policy.

## Exemptions

Any instance where a business area or employee is unable to comply with the requirements of this Policy, an exemption request must be sought in writing from the Policy owner. Exemptions will be considered on a case-by-case basis and will only be granted based on the circumstances and potential risk.
