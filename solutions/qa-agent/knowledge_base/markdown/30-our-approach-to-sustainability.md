## **Listening to our stakeholders** 

## Engaging meaningfully with our stakeholders helps us to better understand and respond to their needs and perspectives. 

## **Understanding stakeholder views on climate change** 

We recognise the importance of balancing diverse stakeholder views to evaluate risks and opportunities, and drive more informed decision making and disclosures, particularly on sustainability issues. We proactively engage with our key stakeholders to understand their perspectives, build trusted relationships, and gather insights that can help shape our products and services. Stakeholder insights also inform our materiality assessment, which identifies the sustainability-related impacts, risks and opportunities most important to our stakeholders and the Bank. 

We acknowledge that climate change is a collective challenge and we actively engage with our stakeholders to understand their diverse perspectives. 

Our key stakeholders are individuals and groups in our value chain whose interests are, or could be, materially impacted by the Bank’s activities. Guided by our values and Code of Conduct, we engage with key stakeholders through our regular business activities and dedicated feedback channels. Stakeholder insights are shared through internal forums and committees to support informed decision making across the Bank. 

## **This includes:** 

**• Engaging with customers and providing products and services to help them transition and build climate resilience.** 

**• Engaging with investors to understand their climate-related priorities. This helps to inform our strategy, reporting and deliver long-term sustainable returns for our shareholders.** 

Engagement with customers, communities, government and regulators helps us to consider opportunities to support financial inclusion and enhance customer outcomes. For example, stakeholder channels such as the CBA Community Council, Indigenous Advisory Council and Modern Slavery Advisory Council connect us to community groups and can help inform our response to issues such as financial abuse, artificial intelligence (AI) and customer vulnerability. 

- **Working with and assessing our supply chain to monitor and reduce supply chain-related emissions.** 

We continue to enhance our stakeholder engagement by improving how insights are shared across the Bank. Stakeholder insights help to inform our operational and strategic priorities, as well as the development of products and services.
