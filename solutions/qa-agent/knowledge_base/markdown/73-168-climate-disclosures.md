## **Energy consumption and use of renewable electricity** 

We are committed to purchasing the equivalent of 100% renewable electricity for our operations by 2030. In 2024, we achieved RE100 certification at 99.6% for the Group, with ASB metering limitations accounting for the remaining 0.4%. Our Australian operations have sourced 100% renewable electricity since January 2020, extending to other overseas operations in 2021 and ASB in New Zealand in 2022. We have also achieved our target of 2 MW of on-site solar capacity across our Australian properties by 2025.
