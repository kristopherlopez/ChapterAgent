For customers captured in sector-level financed emissions goal or target reporting, scope of sector is aligned to sector-level goal or target inclusion criteria. For power generation, thermal coal mining, upstream oil and gas extraction, and heavy industry if a customer is excluded based on the sector inclusion criteria they are reallocated to the respective parent category. For all other customers, customers are reported based on ANZSIC classification.
