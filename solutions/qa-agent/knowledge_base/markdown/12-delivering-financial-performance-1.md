## **Sustainable shareholder returns** 

Delivering strong and sustainable fully franked dividends benefits many Australians. We have over 800,000 direct shareholders and more than 13 million Australians benefit from holding CBA shares indirectly through their superannuation. We have delivered a strong payout ratio of 79% near the upper end of our target range. By maintaining strong performance and prudent balance sheet settings, we are able to fund balance sheet growth, invest for the future and pay a sustainable dividend over the long term.
