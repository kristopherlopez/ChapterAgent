## **Key areas of Board discussion in 2025** 

## **Risk management** 

The Board’s focus included the management of financial risks such as funding and liquidity, and non-financial risks, including fraud and scams, privacy and financial crime compliance. Key activities included: 

- Approving the risk management approach and changes to the risk appetite statement. 

- Overseeing the Bank’s Risk Management Framework, its operation by management, and receiving regular reports on financial and non-financial risk. 

- Approving revised risk-related policies including the Group Conflicts Management Policy, and the annual Group Aggregate Risk Exposures Policy and Group Liquidity Policy reviews. 

- Overseeing updates on implementation of CPS 230. 

- As required by APRA’s Prudential Standard CPS 220 _Risk Management_ (CPS 220), delivering an annual risk management declaration to APRA that the Risk Management Framework is adequately designed and operating effectively. 

- Monitoring the Bank’s risk culture. 

- Noting and discussing the key outcomes of the renewal of the Bank’s insurance program. 

## **Fairer treatment of customers** 

The Board actively monitored a range of customer-related topics and initiatives this year. Key activities included: 

- Monitoring the progress of customer remediation initiatives. 

- Reviewing reporting on CBA’s performance on trust, reputation and brand, considering the link between trust and advocacy, and our broader contribution to the community. 

- Reviewing reports on customer complaints. 

- Monitoring the progress of the activities to reach our superior customer experience ambition. 

- Hearing from the Customer Advocate and the Chair of the Indigenous Advisory Council.

## **Strategy and business performance** 

Strategy is considered at each multi-day Board meeting. Key activities included: 

- Receiving updates on the technology strategy with a focus on AI strategy, which includes data and AI. 

- Reviewing regular reports from the CEO and Executives on business performance. 

- Considering and approving the FY25–27 Business Plan and considering the draft Group FY26–28 Business Plan. 

- Receiving regular updates on acquisition and divestment activity. 

## **People and culture** 

The Board regularly addresses a range of people- and culture-related topics and issues. Key activities included: 

- Approving changes to the Code of Conduct. 

- Approving the diversity, equity and inclusion 2025–2028 strategy, including the gender equity action plan. 

- Considering and discussing reports related to health, safety and wellbeing, including incident trends, significant investigations and assurance activity. 

- Reviewing the Group’s remuneration strategies and remuneration outcomes for the CEO, the CEO’s direct reports and other specified roles. 

- Considering the Executive Remuneration Framework.

## **Key areas of Board discussion in 2025** continued 

## **Financial oversight** 

The Board remained focused on operational excellence and driving growth in its core banking businesses, as well as prudent balance sheet and capital management. Key activities included: 

- Monitoring the Bank’s operating performance. 

- Approving the FY24 full year and FY25 half year financial results. 

- Approving the FY24 final dividend and the FY25 interim dividend. 

- Receiving and considering regular reports from management in relation to the Bank’s funding, capital management and liquidity positions. 

- Noting and discussing simplification and cost strategies. 

## **Environmental and social** 

The Board is responsible for considering the E&S impact of the Bank’s activities, overseeing adherence to the E&S Framework and approving climate-related disclosures. Key activities included: 

- Considering regular updates on the Bank’s E&S program. 

- Approving the 2024 Modern Slavery Statement. 

- Considering updates on the Bank’s social impact, including the Reconciliation Action Plan. 

- Considering and approving the FY26–28 Reconciliation Action Plan.
