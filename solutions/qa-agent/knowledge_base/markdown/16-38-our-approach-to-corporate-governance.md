## **Our corporate governance framework** 

The Board oversees the Bank’s corporate governance framework. The framework is designed to enable sound and prudent management, provide leadership and strategic guidance, and deliver the Bank’s purpose. 

The Board’s responsibilities include setting strategic objectives and risk appetite, and expectations for the Group’s values and desired culture, including approving our Code of Conduct. 

The Board has established four standing Committees to assist it in carrying out its functions. Board Committee roles and responsibilities are detailed in their respective charters. Each Committee makes recommendations to the Board in relation to matters for which they are responsible. Each Board Committee also makes decisions on specific matters, where authorised by the Board to do so.

## **Our Board in action** 

## **Board planning and agenda setting** 

This year the Board held 12 meetings, including six multi-day Board and Committee meetings with structured agendas, six shorter Board meetings and two strategy deep dives, which were held as part of the multi-day Board meetings. Certain CBA Directors also met with non-executive directors of Commonwealth Bank of Australia (Europe) N.V. and ASB, as well as senior executives of CBA India. There is also engagement between Committee Chairs and their equivalent at Commonwealth Securities Limited (CommSec). 

So that the Board and Committees’ time is used efficiently, and discussions reflect the Bank’s priorities, agendas are reviewed by the respective Chairs, in consultation with the Group Company Secretary and the CEO. Forward planners help provide a comprehensive view of upcoming agendas, allowing for adjustments as priorities evolve. The Board retains flexibility for ad hoc matters to be discussed at meetings where appropriate. 

Effective communication between management and the Board ahead of Board and Committee meetings is facilitated through management input into certain agenda items and attending pre-meetings with Committee Chairs and the Group Company Secretary. After Board and Committee meetings, follow up actions are shared to clarify the requests of management. To promote effective decision making and a consistent approach to writing papers, Board and Committee paper templates are available and training on writing papers is offered to preparers, throughout the year. 

## **Overseeing the delivery of our strategy** 

Our governance practices play an essential role in providing oversight of the Bank’s operations and contribute to the development of our strategy. The Board is responsible for endorsing strategic and business unit plans to be implemented by management. This includes approving financial plans, which incorporate strategic and other perspectives. The Board oversees the strategy through Board meetings and strategy deep dives. 

## **Strategy deep dives** 

## **Board meetings** 

The CEO and Executives provide regular reports to the Board. In this way, the Board receives an update on the Group’s performance and the performance of each Executive’s business or support unit. Progress against strategy is a key component of these reports. Standalone agenda items relating to strategic matters are also discussed at each multi-day Board meeting. The topics discussed throughout the year include the Business Plan and reports on customers, business performance, operating environment, acquisitions, technology strategy and environmental strategy. The Board and Committee Chairs are responsible for fostering open, inclusive, and where appropriate, robust discussion and debate. 

The Board held two strategy deep dives this year. These were opportunities for the Board to reflect on the strategy, consider the evolving external environment, review progress against the Bank’s priorities and identify opportunities to do more. This included breakout topic sessions for the Board, as well as input from external subject matter experts. In December, the Directors considered the Bank’s historical and future operating context to inform our refreshed strategy. The refreshed strategy, how we will measure success, key enablers and execution priorities for next year were discussed at the April strategy deep dive. 

## **Oversight of material sustainability-related risks and opportunities** 

The Board receives management reports on sustainability-related topics. For example, this year the Board approved the diversity, equity and inclusion 2025–2028 strategy, including our gender equity action plan. 

The Board also considers the environmental and social (E&S) impact of the Bank’s activities and oversees management of climate risk, within the Bank’s overall business strategy and risk appetite. The Board holds the CEO and ELT accountable for delivering their E&S responsibilities. For example, ahead of review by the Board, the Financial Risk Committee reviewed the results of the annual climate risk materiality assessment.
