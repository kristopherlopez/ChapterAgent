## **ESG Credit Standard** 

We have explicitly incorporated the consideration of ESG risk assessments into our credit risk framework through our ESG Credit Standard. The standard provides a structured framework for the way in which E&S risks are to be considered in credit risk assessment processes for our customers. Along with all our other group-wide credit standards, it establishes the minimum credit expectations and requirements for identifying, managing and monitoring a borrower or counterparty’s E&S risks. The standard, which is applicable across the Bank, requires that existing or new non-retail borrowers or counterparties that meet certain criteria be considered against the commitments in the Bank’s E&S Framework when performing a credit assessment. Throughout the credit process, E&S risks identified are assessed for their impact on our customers’ ability to repay. In some cases, the standard also requires reviewing the borrower’s collateral they pledge to the Bank for E&S risks. 

We have not currently identified a globally accepted, standardised methodology for quantifying climate-related impacts on credit risk. Therefore, our ESG credit assessments continue to be driven by customer due diligence and qualitative assessments in processes tailored to the risk of specific portfolios. We expect our approach to integrating ESG into our credit risk framework will continue to evolve and adapt as industry practices mature, and as regulatory and stakeholder expectations change.

## **Risk appetite statement** 

The Board-approved RAS defines the maximum risk allowable to deliver the Bank’s strategic priorities. Management is required to operate within risk appetite. We have integrated two quantitative RAS indicators to help minimise potential credit losses from portfolios with higher exposure to climate-related risks. These focus on physical risk in the retail portfolio and transition risk in the institutional portfolio. 

For home loans, our indicator measures the percentage of loans with high loan-to-value ratios and exposure to climate physical risks like floods, fires and cyclones. This helps monitor the likelihood of higher credit losses if property values fall in areas experiencing extreme weather. ## **Climate scenario analysis** 

Climate-related risk is highly uncertain. We perform scenario analysis, as needed, to understand the potential future impacts of climate-related risks on our financial position, performance, cash flows and business model. It may also help us make decisions to manage our risks which may include updates to policies, changes to risk appetite settings, portfolio limits or exclusions and consideration in capital planning. We acknowledge some of our customers, communities and regions will face greater climate impacts and risks than others, and we remain well positioned to support them.
