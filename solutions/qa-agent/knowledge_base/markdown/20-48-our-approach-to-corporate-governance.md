## **Board education and training** 

Providing Directors with opportunities to enhance their skills and knowledge is essential for them to perform effectively. 

The Nominations Committee is responsible for overseeing the Non-Executive Director induction program and appropriate continuing education opportunities for Directors, as well as periodically reviewing whether there is a need for existing Directors to undertake additional professional development. 

## **Key components of Director training and education** 

**Induction program** Assists new Non-Executive Directors in understanding the Bank’s operations including governance, risk management, technology strategy and cyber security, E&S issues, audit, and regulatory and compliance matters. This year new Non-Executive Directors, Kate Howitt and Alistair Currie, attended customised induction programs, delivered as part of a multi-stage process. **Continuing** Incorporated into the Board calendar to enable Directors to develop and **education program** maintain skills and knowledge that support the Board’s decision making. To upskill our Board, targeted education sessions were provided which included presentations from external subject matter experts. Over the last 12 months this has covered topics such as Australia’s energy transition, technology modernisation and CPS 230. In addition, the Board participated in an educational trip to Asia to explore topics relevant to CBA’s context. This is an important part of the Board’s annual strategy calendar, serving as a source of new ideas and learning. **Annual directors’** Provided to all Directors. **duties training** This year included a refresher on key directors’ duties and evolving areas such as cyber, AI and CPS 230. Additional training was also provided on the Financial Accountability Regime, whistleblowing and key regulatory obligations. **Group mandatory** Directors are required to complete training on Group policies. In 2025 this **learning** included topics such as financial crime compliance, privacy, workplace health and safety, information security including cyber security, our Code of Conduct and conflicts of interest. 

## **Board skills and experience** 

The Matrix sets out the skills and experience considered essential to the effectiveness of the Board and its Committees. It is used to guide the Board renewal process. 

Each year the Directors, including the Chair, self-assess their individual skills and experience. Self-assessment ratings and the Matrix are reviewed by the Nominations Committee, which makes recommendations for approval by the Board.
