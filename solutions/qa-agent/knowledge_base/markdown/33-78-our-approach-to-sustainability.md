## **Supporting community organisations** 

Through the CommBank Staff Foundation, volunteering and other charitable and employee giving initiatives, we have provided support to hundreds of community organisations. The grants program helps relieve cost pressures for community organisations such as those supporting mental health, victim-survivors of domestic violence, children’s health, migrants and refugees, and more.

## **Enabling First Nations economic aspirations** 

The Bank has a longstanding commitment to reconciliation, having launched our first Reconciliation Action Plan (RAP) in 2008. Through the RAP program, Reconciliation Australia supports organisations to take meaningful action to contribute to reconciliation. Through our FY26-28 RAP, we seek to be a trusted partner to First Nations peoples as they achieve their social, cultural and economic aspirations. Our RAP focuses on strengthening relationships with First Nations communities, providing meaningful career opportunities, supporting First Nations business growth, and improving access to banking products and services. In recognition of our contribution to reconciliation, our FY26-28 RAP received Elevate status from Reconciliation Australia. 

Since 2008, we have improved our understanding of the role we can play in reconciliation. We recognise that initiatives are more effective and impactful when First Nations peoples are leading solutions and are involved in the design and delivery of initiatives. We have worked with our Indigenous Leadership Team and Indigenous Advisory Council to develop a set of principles that aim to strengthen our engagement with First Nations peoples. Over the next three years, we will work to apply these principles to the way we deliver our reconciliation priorities as set out in the RAP.

## **First Nations Reach Program** 

**We want to improve access to basic banking services for remote First Nations customers and communities to support their financial inclusion. Our teams, supported by First Nations employees, work with local organisations to share the program’s purpose, better understand community needs and offer banking support. Since the program’s launch in March 2024, we have supported over 2,300 customers across 22 locations covering 32 remote communities in Australia, including Wurrumiyanga in the Tiwi Islands, and Pormpuraaw and Kowanyama in the Cape York Peninsula. During our visits, our people help open accounts, including for children under the age of 14, help restore access to banking services, resolve identification issues, and assist with fraud and other enquiries.**

## **Investing in next generation football talent** 

**We continue to support the Growing Football Fund in partnership with Football Australia. This year an additional 118 community clubs and associations were awarded grants to fund initiatives aimed at driving women and girls’ participation in grassroots football. The Growing Football Fund is on track to achieve its ambition of supporting the attraction and retention of 50,000 female participants by 2026, with record growth in women and girls’ participation in the sport. It has also supported a range of coaching initiatives focused on upskilling community coaches. This year we extended and expanded our partnership with Football Australia, which will see CommBank become the naming rights partner for the Socceroos, joining the CommBank Matildas.**
