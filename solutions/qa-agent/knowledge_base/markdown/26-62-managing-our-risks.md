62 MANAGING OUR RISKS 

## **Risk management oversight** 

The Board has ultimate responsibility for the Bank’s risk governance, including the RMF and oversight of its operation by management. 

The Board is supported by the Risk & Compliance Committee, Audit Committee, Nominations Committee and People & Remuneration Committee, as well as specific management-level committees that oversee material risks. Each year the Board assesses the maturity of our RMF in managing our material risks through our RMD, and adjusts the RMF as required. Together with the setting of risk appetite, this helps the Bank maintain a risk management framework that is fit for our business size, operating context and delivery of our strategic priorities. Our RMD to APRA allows the Board to reflect on the framework’s design and operating effectiveness and adjust, where needed. 

Committees assist the Board to fulfil its roles and responsibilities. Committees meet to consider and discuss specific matters in detail and, where appropriate, make recommendations to the Board for resolution. Processes are in place to support effective and timely information flows between Committees and provide the Board with clear oversight of matters. 

**For more information on our corporate governance structure and committees, see page 38.**

## **Risk Management Declaration** 

**Each year the Board makes a RMD to APRA to meet the requirements of APRA’s CPS 220, confirming the RMF remains fit for managing our material risks, or outlines steps to strengthen the RMF. Lines 1 and 2, with an opinion from Line 3 and collective agreement by the ELT, form a recommendation on the RMD to the Board Risk & Compliance Committee. This supports the Directors to determine whether the RMF is adequately designed and operating effectively in all material respects. This year particular attention was given to protecting our customers through strengthening our RMF to help reduce fraud, scams, cyber threats, financial crime, and business disruption and to support the Bank’s responsible use of AI. This focus reflects the evolving threat landscape and reinforces our commitment to strengthening the resilience of our organisation to deliver our strategy.** 

## **Our risk management committees** 

At management level, risk governance is undertaken by a structured hierarchy of individual accountabilities, personal delegations, management committees and forums across the Bank. Management regularly monitors financial, strategic and non-financial risk exposures against the Bank’s RAS and policy limits. This information is provided to the Board Committees in the form of Board papers, which are prepared and shared with the Board Committees on a bi-monthly basis. Following management committee meetings, papers are shared with Board Committees for noting, approval or discussion. This flow of information is essential for management committees to effectively support the Board.

## **Technology modernisation** 

## **Executive Leadership Team** 

The ELT is accountable for developing and executing the Bank’s strategy and supports the Board by advising on strategic risks and opportunities, including those related to technology. This year the ELT increased its focus on accelerating the modernisation of the Bank’s technology estate to strengthen technology stability, security and operational continuity. Modernisation also supports the use of new tools, technologies and AI, and delivery of enhanced customer experiences. The ELT has set the strategic direction, allocated the required capacity and capability, and maintained oversight of progress against execution priorities. 

**Chaired by: Chief Executive Officer** 

**Meets: Weekly or as required** 

The ELT is responsible for making specific recommendations to the CEO and agreeing common actions addressing strategy, business performance, people leadership and culture, and risk and compliance management and control. The ELT may establish committees to assist it in carrying out its functions.

## **Financial risks** 

## **Group Credit Risk Framework** 

## **Financial Risk Committee** 

The FRC supports the Board by overseeing the Bank’s financial risk frameworks with oversight of the governance, key credit risk principles and policies that apply to the Group. On a continuing basis during the year, various discussion papers are submitted to the FRC for review in response to emerging financial risks and changes to the credit environment, including but not limited to, global geopolitical developments that may impact the Bank and its customers. The committee assesses whether to make adjustments to our risk appetite and/or the Group Credit Risk Framework. 

**Chaired by: Chief Risk Officer** 

**Meets: Monthly** 

The Financial Risk Committee (FRC) assists and advises the CEO on the governance, optimisation and effective management of the Bank’s financial risks in accordance with the RAS and RMF. 

## **Asset and Liability Committee** 

**Chaired by: Chief Financial Officer Meets: Monthly** 

The Asset and Liability Committee (ALCO) oversees the Group’s balance sheet settings and the effective management of the Bank’s non-traded market risk, interest rate risk, liquidity, funding, capital and structural foreign exchange risk.

## **Operational resilience and CPS 230** 

## **Non-Financial Risk Committee** 

The NFRC discussed requirements of CPS 230, including Board and management accountabilities, and plans to achieve compliance. Leading up to the effective date of 1 July 2025, the Committee reviewed the Business Continuity Plan summaries, including associated tolerances of the Bank’s critical operations ahead of review by the Board Risk & Compliance Committee. 

**Chaired by: Chief Executive Officer Meets: Bi-monthly** 

The Non-Financial Risk Committee (NFRC) advises the CEO on the governance, optimisation and effective management of the Bank’s non-financial risks in accordance with the RAS and RMF. 

## **Risk & Remuneration Review Committee** 

**Chaired by: Chief Risk Officer Meets: Quarterly** 

The Group Risk & Remuneration Review Committee advises the Group Chief Risk Officer on risk considerations for determining remuneration outcomes in line with the Group Remuneration Policy. This assists the Group Chief Risk Officer to support the Chief Executive Officer and Board to determine remuneration outcomes across the Bank.
