## **Hearing directly from our customers** 

**To better understand customer needs, the Board met with customers in Adelaide across sectors such as construction, building supplies, engineering, energy, retail and agriculture. These discussions offered valuable insights into our customers’ business operations, challenges, growth opportunities and what their expectations may be of CBA.** 

## **Board performance, composition and renewal** 

The Board recognises the importance of having Directors with an appropriate mix of skills, experience and diversity to support sound decision making. 

As at 30 June 2025, the Board had 10 Directors: nine independent Non-Executive Directors and the CEO. With the assistance of the Nominations Committee, the Board regularly reviews its size and composition, and considers factors including independence, skills, experience and diversity of views. 

## **Board renewal** 

Board renewal and orderly transitions are important for ensuring effective and sustainable Board performance. The Board skills matrix and the CBA Board Appointment, Renewal and Performance Policy frame the ongoing Board renewal process. These work together so that prescribed skills and experience are present within the Board and address the Bank’s existing and emerging strategic, business and governance issues. 

The Board remains focused on ongoing renewal and succession. Consideration is given to diversity, background, experience and skills. There have been several changes to the composition of the Board and certain Committees this year. 

Kate Howitt was appointed as an independent Non-Executive Director from 1 October 2024. Kate has significant experience in matters relating to strategy, capital allocation, stakeholder management and sustainability. 

Alistair Currie was appointed as an independent Non-Executive Director from 31 March 2025. Alistair has extensive international banking experience, having held roles in Australia, the United Kingdom, the United States, Asia and the Middle East. 

On 16 October 2024, Anne Templeman-Jones retired from the Board as an independent Non-Executive Director, Chair of the Audit Committee and member of the Risk & Compliance Committee. 

Following the retirement of Anne Templeman-Jones, Peter Harmer commenced as Chair of the Audit Committee, member of the Risk & Compliance Committee and stepped down from his role as a member of the People & Remuneration Committee. Lyn Cobley was subsequently appointed as a member of the People & Remuneration Committee. 

## **Board performance evaluation** 

Continuously monitoring and improving its performance, as well as that of its Committees and individual Directors, remains important to the Board. Under its Charter, the Board must annually assess its performance and the performance of individual Directors. Additionally, an external performance review of the Board and Committees is required at least every three years. 

In 2024, an independent external evaluation assessed the performance of the Board, each Committee and individual Directors. The process included observation of Board meetings, and the completion of a survey and interviews with Directors, Executives, the Group Auditor and the Group Company Secretary. It also evaluated the effectiveness of Board and Committee processes, the appropriateness of meeting agendas, and the timeliness and adequacy of papers. The findings were presented to the Board in August 2024. 

An internal review was carried out in 2025. The process involved the completion of a survey by Directors, Executives, the Group Auditor and the Group Company Secretary covering matters such as roles and responsibilities, effectiveness, engagement and reporting. A performance review report was prepared and provided to the Board. The Chair also met with each of the participating Directors to share two-way feedback.
