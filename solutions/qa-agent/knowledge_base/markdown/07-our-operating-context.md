## **Our operating context** 

We operate in a dynamic environment shaped by diverse stakeholders and evolving trends. This can influence our strategic priorities, decision-making processes and overall risk management approach. 

## **Key themes this year** 

Across the Bank, we regularly assess the external environment to understand stakeholder views and evolving themes. This helps us refine our strategic priorities, respond to risks and opportunities, and support informed decision making to deliver sustainable outcomes for our stakeholders. 

## **Macroeconomic and cost-of-living pressures** 

Australia’s economy has continued to show resilience, supported by strong population growth along with healthy labour market conditions. While overall conditions remain strong, some household budgets, particularly for younger Australians, renters and mortgage holders, continue to face pressures from elevated interest rates, rising essential costs and housing affordability challenges. 

During the year inflation continued to ease within the Reserve Bank of Australia’s (RBA) target range allowing the RBA to start reducing official interest rates. While economic growth continues, rising public sector expenditure and low levels of productivity growth highlight the growing need for long-term economic reform. 

We maintain a conservative balance sheet, with capital, funding, and credit settings carefully managed to ensure resilience and ongoing support for our customers and the broader economy. 

## **Heightened geopolitical risks** 

The global geopolitical environment continues to change with heightened tensions and policy uncertainty weighing on trade, energy and investment markets. Increasing global trade restrictions, strategic competition and regional conflicts are disrupting supply chains, limiting access to key technologies and reshaping capital flows. Cyber threats and attacks continue to evolve, requiring heightened vigilance by people and businesses. 

Australia’s banking system remains resilient. We actively assess downside scenarios through robust stress testing, scenario analysis, and balance sheet risk management to strengthen operational resilience and safeguard against potential shocks. The Bank is well positioned to navigate external challenges and continue supporting our customers and the broader economy. 

## **Competitive intensity** 

Competition in the banking sector remains elevated, driven by rising customer expectations, digital innovation and industry participants pursuing greater market share. Customers are increasingly seeking personalised, high-value experiences and seamless service. Pricing pressure remains evident across deposits and lending, where customer demand for value and changing channel dynamics contribute to a highly competitive environment. We continue to see large technology firms expand their presence, especially in payments and small business segments. As more customers engage through digital channels, delivering efficient, high-quality service at scale to all Australians is essential. Continued investment in technology and innovation helps us to meet these expectations while maintaining our operational resilience. 

Maintaining our position as Australia’s leading bank requires a consistent focus on our customers – building trusted relationships and continuing to invest in technology, data, and insights to deliver personalised and value-added experiences. 

## **Evolving technology** 

Advances in AI, data analytics and digital infrastructure are transforming the banking landscape. These technologies offer opportunities to enhance customer experience, streamline processes, and improve risk management and operational efficiency. At the same time, they introduce new risks – including cyber security threats, scams, privacy concerns and the use of AI. 

Rapidly adopting technology such as AI to modernise existing industries and build new industries is critical for our economy. Careful consideration will be needed to incentivise productive investment, build sovereign capability and retain economic benefits in Australia for all Australians. 

We continue to invest in modernising our technology platforms and strengthening cyber resilience to safeguard operations and provide secure, reliable services. We are committed to managing technology risks and engaging regulators to support safer and responsible innovation. Ongoing investment in technology, data, and our people is essential to maintaining trust, building long-term strength, and staying ahead of technology-led change. 

## **Trust and reputation** 

Trust is fundamental to long-term value creation and building strong customer relationships. Many Australians and Australian businesses trust us with their transaction banking and deposit relationship. This requires a continued focus on being safe and strong. As a bank, we are inherently exposed to reputational risk with issues such as data privacy, conduct and governance having the potential to quickly erode stakeholder confidence. 

Expectations continue to rise around transparency, the responsible use of data and AI, and the delivery of fairer and responsible outcomes. Stakeholders are also increasingly focused on how we support Australia’s economy and contribute to financial inclusion and broader community outcomes. 

The Bank has an important role to play. We are working closely with government and industry to support more sustainable outcomes for our customers and communities. These efforts are fundamental to maintaining financial system stability and resilience, and to supporting the long-term health of the Australian economy and society.
