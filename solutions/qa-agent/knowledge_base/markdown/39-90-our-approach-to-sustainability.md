## **Vigilance on cyber security** 

Cyber resilience and information security remain key priorities for the Bank. As cyber threats become more frequent and complex, we continue to invest in strategies, advanced technologies such as AI and GenAI, and skilled teams to help protect our customers, assets and data. 

This year we enhanced the resilience of our cloud infrastructure and improved key cyber control operations. For example, we implemented near real-time code scanning to help detect and mitigate risks associated with sensitive information in our code. We are adopting AI tools to help detect and neutralise external threats more efficiently. We also worked with our suppliers to enhance their data and system security, helping to further strengthen our own resilience. 

To help support system-wide resilience and strengthen the national security ecosystem, we engage closely with Government and industry. Alongside industry partners, we contributed to a consultation process that led to the introduction of Australia’s first standalone cyber security law – the _Cyber Security Act 2024._ We also collaborated with the 

Tech Council of Australia and the Group of Eight Australian Universities on the inaugural Australian Cyber Exchange 2025, which aimed to showcase and strengthen Australia’s sovereign cyber security capabilities. 

We continue to strengthen our cyber resilience by regularly reviewing and testing our approach, as well as engaging external firms to assess and provide feedback. We look to integrate insights from these assessments into our risk mitigation strategies to help enhance our protective and defensive capabilities. 

We consider cyber security a shared responsibility across the Bank. Our people play a key role by helping build secure processes and products, and effectively identifying and responding to cyber threats. We have built a large and capable security team of approximately 1,400 people and actively support developing the next generation of cyber talent.

## **Safeguarding customer privacy** 

We have a responsibility to protect the personal information of our customers. We apply processes and controls to respect and protect our customers’ personal information, aiming to prevent data breaches and responsibly govern personal information in accordance with our internal privacy policy. Our public Group Privacy Statement provides transparency on how the Bank collects and handles personal information, in compliance with privacy laws. 

Following the enactment of the _Privacy Act 1988_ reforms in December 2024, we are working to update our Group Privacy Statement to provide greater transparency on how we use personal information for our automated decision-making processes, including the types of personal information used. We continue to enhance our personal information management processes through periodic reviews of our RMF, and updates to internal policies and standards. For suppliers who collect or handle customer personal information, we have processes to assess whether their data and privacy governance, policies and incident response measures are in line with our responsibilities. 

Recognising the ever-evolving challenges of safeguarding personal information, we are committed to meeting our regulatory requirements, with customer and community expectations in mind.

## **Keeping our customers safe** 

**Keeping our customers safe is a priority. This year we commenced rollout of multi-factor authentication (MFA) as an added layer of protection against unauthorised access to customers’ accounts and to keep their money safe. We began our phased rollout of MFA in April 2025 to our NetBank registered customers who are also CommBank app registered. Once enrolled, each time a customer logs into online banking, they will need to confirm the login attempt through our CommBank app.**

## **Modernising our data infrastructure** 

Protecting and serving our customers begins with how we manage data – one of our most valuable and differentiating assets. Access to good quality data means we can make better decisions faster, mitigate risks, and deliver more innovative and personalised customer experiences. It also serves as a key input in our AI solutions. Our ambition is to make it easier for our people to securely access data faster, enabling them to unlock greater value from data. This year we have made progress modernising our data technology and used AI to reduce complexity and duplication in our data. 

## **Combatting financial crime** 

As a major financial institution, we play a key role in combatting financial crime to help protect the integrity of the financial system, our customers and communities. We continue to invest in detection and case management capabilities to better assess risk across customers, channels and products, while strengthening our AML/CTF compliance to meet regulatory obligations in Australia and other jurisdictions. To support identification and management of risk, we systematically review guidance from regulators, intergovernmental bodies such as the Financial Action Task Force, and non-government resources to understand and address emerging financial crime trends and typologies. 

## **Raising scam awareness** 

**Money mule scams are an increasing form of financial crime in Australia, particularly targeting students and young people. This year CBA partnered with media company Campus Group to raise awareness on the scams across 13 university campuses nationwide. The campaign focused on educating students about the risks and consequences of mule scams, helping them recognise and avoid potential threats.** 

Alongside industry partners, we continue to contribute to the Fintel Alliance’s efforts to combat money laundering, terrorism financing and other serious crimes. This year we worked with AUSTRAC and other major banks to analyse 56 million data points under $10,000, uncovering criminal patterns that would be difficult for individual institutions to detect alone. 

To better protect our community from financial crime, we introduced QR Cardless functionality in the CommBank app to enable secure, card-free ATM transactions. This feature eliminates shared credentials, verifies identity for every transaction, and uses behavioural and geolocation analytics to detect suspicious activity. 

## **Faster financial crime assessments using AI in the Business Bank** 

**We are actively exploring how AI can support our teams in combating financial crime. This year we deployed an AI-driven solution to accelerate early detection and response in financial crime risk assessment. The model enhances prioritisation of potential cases in the Business Bank, enabling analysts to concentrate on high-risk threats. Initial deployments of the model have demonstrated an improvement in detection, strengthening our analytical capabilities, and enhancing protection for customers and the broader community. This initiative reflects our commitment to innovation and safeguarding stakeholder trust.**
