## **Progressing our culture** 

**==> picture [193 x 236] intentionally omitted <==**

We continue to monitor and evaluate progress against our culture and customer ambition. Our annual organisational culture assessment and action plan helps us identify opportunities to safely deliver improvements for customers, sooner. 

One of our priority cultural initiatives is focused on connecting all our people with customers. To support our pursuit of excellence in customer experience, we are developing opportunities that will allow more of our people to connect with customers, helping them understand how they can contribute to improving customer experiences. We are expanding our branch visitations program to help our people who are not in customer-facing roles visit, observe and interact with our frontline teams to gain a deeper understanding of our customers’ needs, experiences and challenges. 

We are also building more reflection points into our ways of working to help us learn from both successes and setbacks. This allows for courage in sharing experiences, deeper curiosity, and committing to actions to enhance our customer focus and get better every day. We recognise that effective teams have adaptability and drive to turn every challenge or success into an opportunity for growth.

## **Listening to our people** 

We are always looking for ways to enhance our people’s experience at work and understand how we help our people deliver the greatest impact for our customers, communities and shareholders. We seek regular feedback from our people through various channels and forums, including employee research panels, focus groups and surveys. Our people and culture surveys include an annual culture deep dive, fortnightly pulses and team-led listening as needed. Leaders and teams receive insights about culture, employee experience and the core behaviours to deliver better customer outcomes. In our most recent survey, people engagement was 85%, an increase from last year’s result of 84% in May 2024.

To continue improving our customers’ experiences and maintain our leading main financial institution position, we need to be more ambitious in how we pursue improvements for our customers. ## **Recognising our people** 

**Our annual excellence awards recognise our people who reflect our values and leadership principles, and the behaviours, including risk management, that will help us achieve our customer ambition. The excellence awards align with our cultural priorities, recognising breakthrough outcomes, impactful learning and customer excellence.** 

**One of this year’s award winners, Gijo Mathew, was recognised for his role in rebuilding an automated verification system for home loans and accelerating the digital home loan approval process, creating a much better experience for our customers and people.**
