## **CBA scale, performance and remuneration relative to comparator benchmarks** 

CBA has outperformed consistently against Australian peer banks and is materially larger than the majority of comparative metrics, including size (market capitalisation) and scale (main financial institution (MFI)) as detailed in the graphs below. Alongside ANZ, NAB and WBC, Macquarie Banking and Financial Services (MBFS), a division of the Macquarie Group (MQG), is considered as an additional peer in consumer and business banking. 

## **ASX and offshore comparator groups** 

While the primary benchmark for assessing remuneration competitiveness is Australian peer banks, the ASX[ 1] and offshore[ 2 ] comparator groups are supplementary reference points. 

FR and TMR increases for the executive KMP are designed to position CBA’s remuneration more competitively against domestic peers and maintain current relativity with offshore markets.

Our ELT have contributed to CBA’s consistent and sustained high performance, which is reflected in strong TSR against Australian peer banks (ANZ, MQG, NAB and WBC) demonstrated over the last five years. 

The graphs below present a comparison of CBA CEO’s TMR relative to the supplementary ASX and offshore comparator groups. While there is generally a correlation between TMR and market capitalisation (see trendline), the data indicates that the CBA CEO’s remuneration is positioned more conservatively when compared to the supplementary peer groups, even following the remuneration increase effective FY25. 

Relative to the ASX comparator group, CBA ranks first in market capitalisation while the CEO’s TMR is below median (ranked 12th). Although CBA’s market capitalisation is similar to HSBC and significantly exceeds that of its offshore peers, CBA CEO’s TMR remains considerably lower.
