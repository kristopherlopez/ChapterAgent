## **Acute physical risk in our Australian home loans portfolio** 

To help us effectively manage our climate-related risks, we monitor the impact of severe weather events and natural disasters on our business and customers, including in our home lending portfolio. We use third-party datasets and model providers to help enable a more complete view of the potential risk exposures across our portfolios. This year our analysis indicated CBA exposures of:

Our home loans portfolio is geographically spread across Australia. Homeowners in some areas are highly exposed to acute physical risk and severe weather events such as floods, cyclones and bushfires. This year we sought to more accurately quantify our potential exposure to extreme weather events using a case study of severe cyclones impacting South-East Queensland (QLD). 

We continue to develop and enhance our view of acute physical climate risk by exploring new datasets and improving our understanding of the pathways for credit losses as well as the implications of underinsurance in the home loans portfolio. In the event our customers are affected by natural disasters, such as bushfires, floods or storms, they may be able to access our emergency assistance support options.
