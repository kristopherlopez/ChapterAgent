## **Bankwest transformed to a digital bank** 

**With many Australians preferring a simple digital-only banking experience, we have transformed Bankwest to become a digital bank. Bankwest’s product offering is now simpler with a focus on home loans and everyday banking.** 

**This year we relaunched Bankwest with a new brand, website and mobile app experience. Our new digital assets are designed to be simpler and more intuitive. We also updated the user interface of our award-winning digital broker portal. Bankwest is well positioned to meet the expectations of digital-savvy customers who expect leading digital and broker banking services. To support this, we introduced single and multi-use virtual cards in the Bankwest app. Our investments in technology and modern cloud-based call centre platforms are helping us engage new-to-bank customers.** 

**While closing our Bankwest branch network and converting 15 branches to be CBA-branded, we maintained consistent growth, acquiring over 90,000 new-to-bank customers this year. We also minimised impact to our people by presenting branch employees with job opportunities across the Bank. For around 2,000 Bankwest customers who we identified as being reliant on branches, we have supported them with exploring alternative ways to do their banking. Bankwest customers still have access to cash services via CBA IDMs/ATMs nationwide for depositing and withdrawing cash.**

## **Modernising technology and data** 

## **Advancing our technology leadership** 

Modernising our technology and data platforms is critical to delivering better customer, risk and resilience outcomes. We have accelerated our modernisation efforts. Our ambition is to continue building modern technology by reducing system interdependencies and enhancing our enterprise data platform to support our important customer interactions. This work aims to improve our operational resilience and make it faster to deliver change. We have successfully migrated our on-premise data platforms to the cloud, and are continuing to invest in building microservices enabling a simpler, more modern technology estate. 

**To strengthen our technology and AI leadership and reimagine customer experiences, we have established a Tech Hub in Seattle.** 

**The Hub is providing our teams with the opportunity to spend three weeks in Seattle, focused on solving clearly defined customer and business challenges. During this time, CBA technology teams work with global technology leaders including Amazon Web Services and Microsoft to solve the business problem. Teams are expected to define a customer-centred opportunity, then develop a solution using AI technology.** 

We continue to invest in the security and resilience of our technology and strive to provide services that are reliable, safe, and available whenever and wherever our customers need them. We have taken steps to improve the reliability and availability of our systems to deliver more updates while reducing impact on customers.
