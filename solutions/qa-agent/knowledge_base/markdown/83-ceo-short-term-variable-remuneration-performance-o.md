## **Relative TSR** 

- Relative TSR provides a robust and easily quantifiable performance measure with strong alignment to shareholder value.

- Relative TSR compares the ranking of CBA’s TSR over the performance period with the TSR of other companies in a peer group.

## Risk assessment in performance and remuneration 

**CBA’s performance and remuneration frameworks** support effective risk management by holding employees individually and collectively accountable for managing role-related risks, encouraging behaviour that aligns with the Group’s values, performance management framework, and Code of Conduct.
