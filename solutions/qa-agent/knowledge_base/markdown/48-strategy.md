## **Strategy** 

Our purpose is to build a brighter future for all, inspiring us to support Australia’s transition to a more prosperous, resilient and sustainable future. Our climate strategy aims to deliver on our purpose.

## **Supporting Australia’s transition to net zero by 2050** 

In helping build Australia’s future economy, our climate-related opportunities are centred on supporting our customers to transition to a lower-carbon economy and in helping them make their homes, businesses or activities more resilient to the impacts of climate change. We also aim to continue assessing, managing and monitoring our climate-related risks, and investing in our direct operations to reduce our environmental impact. 

While the financial impacts of climate change for CBA may be less immediate and significant when compared to other material issues, we have developed initiatives which aim to address climate-related risks and opportunities. Our climate strategy includes mitigation and adaptation actions. Current opportunities include: developing specific banking products and services to support customers to reduce their emissions and finance their transition or adaptation initiatives; supporting customers with natural disaster recovery; helping customers to assess their climate risks and develop adaptation strategies; and collaborating with stakeholders to develop tools, insights and research that can be shared. 

We set our first operational emissions targets in 2009. To further support our climate strategy, in 2022 we set our first sector-level financed emissions targets. We take meeting our commitments seriously and are pleased to have met our 2025 Scope 1 and 2, and Scope 3 operational emissions reductions targets. We are also below the reference scenario for 12 of our sector-level financed emissions goals and targets. 

**For important information on how we identify climate-related risks and opportunities, see pages 154–158.**
