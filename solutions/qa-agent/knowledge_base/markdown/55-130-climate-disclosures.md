## **Managing our operational emissions** 

Building on our progress and using insights from reducing our operational emissions, we are now focused on reducing our supply chain emissions. This year we have achieved our operational emissions targets for 2025 and also completed a comprehensive review of our operational emissions strategy covering targets, boundaries and inventories. 

## **Progress on our operational emissions reduction targets** 

Since 2014, we have achieved a 96% reduction in Scope 1 and 2 operational emissions. As at 30 June 2025, we have achieved our operational emissions targets set for 2025 with a Scope 1 and 2 reduction of 72%, and a Scope 3 reduction of 36%, since 2020. 

We set our current Scope 1 and 2, and Scope 3 operational emissions targets in 2021. In 2024, our Scope 3 operational emissions target was updated to align with a pathway to limit global warming to 1.5°C for all Scope 3 operational emissions categories included with our target, except for air travel which remained aligned to a well-below 2°C pathway. We also updated our Scope 3 baseline due to material business structure changes, including divestments, that had taken place since developing the initial baseline.
