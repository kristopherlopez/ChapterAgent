## **1. Basis of preparation** 

The climate disclosures relate to Commonwealth Bank of Australia (CBA) and its subsidiaries (together the Group or the Bank). CBA includes activities provided under the Bankwest and Unloan brands, unless otherwise stated. Our wholly owned subsidiary, ASB Bank Ltd (ASB), is not included within our climate-related processes and disclosures, unless otherwise stated. ASB is required to report in line with New Zealand’s regulatory climate reporting regime. ASB has its own governance, strategy, risk management, metrics, targets and processes for managing its climate-related risks and opportunities. This information can be found in ASB’s Sustainability Report and Climate Statement. For climate metrics, goals and targets included in our climate disclosures any significant exclusions are noted within our reporting boundaries on page 391 or are noted within the report. 

The climate disclosures section contains climate-related metrics and exposures that relate to the full year ended 30 June 2025 with comparisons to the full year ended 30 June 2024, unless otherwise stated. Financed emissions and sector-level goal and target progress are lagged due to customer emissions reporting cadences, and are therefore for the full year ended 30 June 2024 with comparisons to the full year ended 30 June 2023. 

The Bank’s climate disclosures have been prepared with reference to incoming Australian mandatory climate reporting standards and market practice. Our focus for the next 12 months will be on preparing for compliance with mandatory climate reporting obligations, which applies to our reporting period beginning on 1 July 2025. 

We have applied the operational control approach to define our organisational boundary for our greenhouse gas emissions inventory. Our greenhouse gas emissions inventory is prepared with reference to the GHG Protocol Corporate Accounting and Reporting Standard, GHG Protocol Corporate Value Chain (Scope 3 Standard), Climate Active Carbon Neutral Standard for Organisations and Partnership for Carbon Accounting for Financials (PCAF) Part A Financed Emissions Standard. Our Australian Scope 1 and 2 emissions are prepared in accordance with the National Greenhouse Gas and Energy Reporting Measurement Determination. For more information, see pages 392–399 and 419–428, respectively. 

The presentation currency is Australian dollars (AUD), which aligns to the presentation currency used in the consolidated financial statements, with amounts disclosed in billion dollars, rounded to the nearest one decimal place, unless otherwise stated. 

Certain derived values (including percentages) are calculated using exact underlying raw data. As a result, these derived values may not sum due to rounding and recalculating these numbers may lead to a different result. 

Timing of climate impacts can vary, for the Bank we define the following time horizons, which have been applied to our climate scenarios and climate risk materiality assessment (CRMA): 

- Current term (within the next 12 months). 

- Short term (~3 years) to align with the Bank’s strategic planning cycle. 

- Medium term (~10 years) to align with the timeframe of when the Network for Greening the Financial System (NGFS) physical impacts begin to diverge for the low versus high warming futures. 

- Long term (~30 years) to align with the Bank’s home loan portfolio where home loans can be contractually agreed to for up to 30 years. 

**Learn more about climate scenario analysis and the CRMA on pages 149–158.** 

## **Changes in comparatives and prior year restatements** 

We may restate previously reported climate-related metrics, goals and targets contained in this report. This may occur in circumstances, including but not limited to, changes in data availability or calculation methodologies, identification of material discrepancies or significant changes in our business model. Where we identify such a circumstance, we assess whether restating prior period information is necessary. This decision is based on whether the circumstances materially affects the metric, alters its interpretation or is needed for consistency across reports. Our assessment of materiality is based on a 5% threshold, generally applied at the total metric level, such as operational emissions or financed emissions. 

Any restatements made will be clearly identified throughout the report, and where a restatement is impractical, this will also be identified. 

**For more information on our financed emissions restatement policy, see pages 393–394.**

## **Assurance** 

PwC has provided limited assurance over CBA’s 30 June 2024 progress towards our 10 sector-level financed emissions goals and five sector-level financed emissions targets and the 30 June 2025 progress towards CBA’s SFT. PwC has also provided reasonable assurance for our total Group Scope 1 and 2 operational emissions and total energy consumption for Australia, and limited assurance for percentage of electricity procured for the Group. Additionally, limited assurance has been provided for selected sustainability metrics in our Australian operations, including water, total waste and office paper used. 

**PwC’s Assurance Reports are available at the end of this report on pages 430–437.**
