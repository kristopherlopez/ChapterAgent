## **Scope 2 emissions** 

Scope 2 operational emissions are indirect emissions from the Group’s purchase of electricity used in retail, commercial, residential and data centre properties, SSTs (ATMs) and charging of the EV fleet under the Group’s operational control during the reporting period. The consumption data is based on a combination of invoiced amounts and estimates based on historical information or pro-rata consumption. Emissions are calculated using the relevant emissions factors noted in this section. This is the Criteria for the accompanying Selected Sustainability Information assured by PwC. 

## **Selected Scope 3 emissions** 

Selected Scope 3 operational emissions are all other indirect emissions (not included in Scope 2) that occur in the value chain of the reporting company. This is the Criteria for the accompanying Selected Sustainability Information assured by PwC. The tables below outline all selected Scope 3 operational emissions sources and the emissions factors used to calculate emissions reported within our Annual Report. 

**For a reconciliation between Scope 3 operational emissions and those within our Scope 3 target, see page 428.** 

We use a location-based and market-based reporting approach. Location-based methodology reflects the average emissions intensity of grids on which energy consumption occurs, while a market-based methodology reflects emissions from electricity that we have purposefully chosen to purchase. 

The tables below outline Scope 2 operational emissions sources reported within our Annual Report.
