## **Our climate-related risk policies, processes and tools** 

Climate-related risk can amplify other risk types. Our approach aims to integrate the identification, assessment and management of climate-related risks into existing business activities to help us understand and manage any changes to our strategic, financial and non-financial risks. 

We use a range of tools and techniques to help us identify, assess, manage and monitor climate-related risks and opportunities to our strategy, our portfolios, and at an individual supplier and customer level where certain criteria are met. This year we continued to enhance our climate risk management tools and practices in line with evolving industry practices and regulatory expectations. The table below outlines the key policies, processes and tools in place to help us identify, assess, manage and monitor our risks and opportunities.

## **Embedding climate into our credit risk framework** 

## **How climate impacts credit risk** 

More frequent, severe weather events and long-term changes in climate patterns can cause damage or decline in asset values, impacting the ability of households and businesses to repay their loans. The ability of some businesses to repay loans could also be impacted by a decline in the viability of their business models and profitability. This could be due to new climate change and energy policies, technological advancements, or changing community expectations that impact demand for their products or services. Unemployment could also increase in regions dependent on these businesses, resulting in increased losses to the Bank from retail customers. Physical and transition factors could also decrease the value of the assets held as collateral by the Bank, resulting in more severe credit losses when a default occurs. It is important we assess and understand the impacts to help us manage our risks effectively.
