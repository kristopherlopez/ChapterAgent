The Audit Committee, People & Remuneration Committee and Risk & Compliance Committee meet six times per year, or more frequently if necessary. The Nominations Committee meets at least four times per year, or more often as needed. 

Typically Committees consider an agenda item in detail and make recommendations to the Board. In certain circumstances a Committee will make a decision independently, such as the People & Remuneration Committee setting and reviewing the fee framework for non-executive directors on certain CBA subsidiary boards. 

Committee charters and forward planners guide the matters considered. This helps to facilitate effective and timely information flows between Committees to assist the Board with clear oversight of matters through to resolution. After each meeting, a report and recommendation paper is provided to the Board. The Chair of each Committee presents the report at the next Board meeting. Minutes of meetings are available to all Directors. 

Concurrent meetings of the four Board Committees occured twice this year to consider remuneration and performance outcomes for Executives[ 1] and performance outcomes for the Group Auditor. Remuneration outcomes for the CEO and Group Executives include the consideration of sustained performance across both financial and non-financial measures, including shareholder, customer, people and leadership, and strategy execution. The concurrent meeting also considers relevant risk and audit matters, and positive and negative risk behaviours, which may result in the upward or downward adjustment, including a reduction to zero, of executive remuneration outcomes. Concurrent meetings occur when the People & Remuneration Committee meets concurrently with the Nominations Committee, Risk & Compliance Committee and Audit Committee. The concurrent meeting also facilitates information sharing on key matters to enable ongoing oversight of risks, behaviours, values, remuneration and performance outcomes for Executives.[ 1]
