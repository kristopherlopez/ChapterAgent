## **Financed emissions portfolio analysis** 

Our calculations cover 96% of our in-scope drawn lending exposure. Movements in the overall absolute financed emissions and emissions intensity of our in-scope drawn lending portfolio are generally dependent on changes in our portfolio mix over time and differences in emissions profiles at the sector or customer level. We estimate our 2024 absolute financed emissions for our in-scope lending portfolio at 22.6 MtCO2-e, an increase of 9% (+1.8 MtCO2-e), compared to the restated 2023 absolute financed emissions estimate (20.8 MtCO2-e). Most notable increases were observed in the transport and storage, and manufacturing sectors. These movements were partly offset by reductions in estimated absolute emissions in the mining sector. Decarbonisation of the grid, reflected in changes in grid intensity factors in our estimates, continued to contribute to emissions decreases in Australian housing and Australian commercial property.[ 1]

## **Use of carbon credits** 

In line with the PCAF Standard, when we refer to our financed emissions this is absolute gross emissions where data allows. We do not currently have plans to purchase carbon credits to achieve our sector-level financed emissions goals and targets. However, we acknowledge that some of our customers may be subject to regulatory regimes that require them to use carbon credits, while others may be voluntarily using credits to offset their emissions as part of their decarbonisation strategies.
