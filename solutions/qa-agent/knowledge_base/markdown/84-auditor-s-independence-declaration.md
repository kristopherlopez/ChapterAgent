## **Auditor’s Independence Declaration** 

As lead auditor for the audit of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia for the year ended 30 June 2025, I declare that to the best of my knowledge and belief, there have been: 

(a) no contraventions of the auditor independence requirements of the _Corporations Act 2001_ in relation to the audit; and 

(b) no contraventions of any applicable code of professional conduct in relation to the audit. 

This declaration is in respect of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia and the entities it controlled during the period.
