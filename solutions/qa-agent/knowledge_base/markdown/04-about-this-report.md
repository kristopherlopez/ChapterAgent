## **About this report** 

Our Annual Report includes material information on the strategic priorities, corporate governance and risk management of the Commonwealth Bank of Australia (CBA) and its subsidiaries (together the Group or the Bank), as well as our financial and non-financial performance. The material themes in this report are informed by our sustainability materiality assessment detailed on pages 72 to 73. We continue to evolve our reporting to align with changes in legislation, market practice and feedback from our stakeholders. 

This year our climate disclosures are included in this report, not in a separate Climate Report. Our climate disclosures focus on decision-useful information regarding the Bank’s approach to climate-related risks and opportunities. We will continue to evolve our climate disclosures in line with new mandatory climate reporting requirements that will apply for future reporting periods. 

## **Reporting boundary** 

Information included in this report relates to the Group. Financial information is consolidated and includes those entities over which we have control. Non-financial information considers key stakeholders in our value chain predominantly in Australia, unless stated otherwise. This report covers the period from 1 July 2024 to 30 June 2025 (this year), or the Group’s 2025 financial year (FY25). It also includes information about material subsequent events occurring after 30 June 2025 and before the Board’s approval of the report on 13 August 2025. The presentation currency is Australian dollars (AUD) with amounts disclosed rounded to the nearest billion dollars, unless otherwise stated.

## **Significant judgements** 

Each year we conduct a qualitative sustainability materiality assessment to identify and disclose against the sustainability-related topics most relevant to our stakeholders in our value chain and to the Bank. The assessment considers sustainability-related impacts, risks and opportunities that have the potential to influence CBA’s ability to create value over the short to medium term (1–5 years). This year’s assessment reviewed a range of sustainability topics, which were prioritised using our 5x5 risk matrix and exercised significant judgement. The prioritised topics were then grouped into overarching themes that informed the structure of this report. Theme selection involved significant judgement, guided by the results of our materiality assessment. 

Sustainability-related information includes non-financial information and refers to environmental, social and governance topics and themes that are important to our stakeholders and to CBA, as they have the potential to impact stakeholders and CBA’s financial prospects in the short to medium term. While some topics are traditionally linked to sustainability, others are included based on their perceived potential to materially impact our stakeholders and CBA’s financial prospects.
