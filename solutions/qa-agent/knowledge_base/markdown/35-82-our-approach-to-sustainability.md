## **Building tomorrow’s workforce today** 

## **Attracting talent and developing key pipelines** 

To help us deliver our strategy we continue to prioritise talent attraction and retention, particularly for critical skills in technology. We are committed to creating a culture of continuous learning so that our people can continue to grow and develop, and we remain a workplace of choice for key talent. To attract and retain the right people, we are differentiating our experience by providing personalised progression with pathways to upskill, reskill and grow careers. 

We are focused on developing talent pipelines, including through our graduate program and our career comeback program. This year we welcomed 350 graduates into the program with over 200 in technology and have expanded our career comeback program into engineering and financial crime. Since 2021, 43 individuals have been placed in our career comeback program with 97% offered employment upon completion. Building awareness and early engagement with students remains a focus, sponsoring community initiatives targeting potential talent through Girls in Tech, She Codes, Day of AI, ABCN Tech Programs, Vogue Codes and CSIRO Young Indigenous Women in STEM programs. Recognising the importance of diverse talent pipelines, we continue to explore alternative pathways to secure entry-level technology talent. We have adopted the T-EDI Standards, a data-driven set of principles to support inclusion in technology. We have also made a commitment with the NSW Digital Skills and Workforce Compact to hire 20% of entry-level technology talent through alternate pathways by 2030. 

## **Investing in future skills** 

Technology and customer expectations continue to reshape our ways of working. We want our people to learn, build and apply the skills that prepare us for the future of work. We continue to empower our people with scaled access to the latest AI products, including more than 17,500 of our people granted access to Copilot. With more of our people now accessing AI tools in their role, it is important that we help them to use the technology responsibly and effectively to drive better customer outcomes. Our approach to upskilling and reskilling remains focused on the technical, behavioural and leadership skills required to deliver our strategy and manage risk.

## **Helping our people thrive** 

## **Reflecting the customers and communities we serve** 

We recognise the value in diversity of thought and having a team that reflects the customers and communities we serve. We want to create an environment where everyone feels included, safe and respected at work. As we work towards this, we are focused on initiatives which strengthen inclusion and prevent behaviours and norms that can normalise disrespect and lead to inequality. Our business unit inclusion and diversity councils and seven employee diversity networks support the execution of our strategy by providing feedback and lived experience. 

This year we reviewed our 2025-2028 Diversity, Equity and Inclusion strategy. We reset and aligned our diversity goals across gender equality, Indigenous workforce representation and cultural diversity. While we have made progress, there is more to do. We have set 2028 as the target date for these refreshed goals. 

We are working towards achieving 47-50% gender equality in Executive Manager and above roles, having reached 45.1% this year. Our goal of 3% Aboriginal and/or Torres Strait Islander representation in our domestic workforce remains a focus, with current representation at 1.4%. We are also aiming for 40% cultural diversity in Executive Manager and above roles, with representation at 39.0% this year. 

The Bank remains committed to improving gender equality outcomes. We continue to see a lower proportion of women in senior and higher-paying technical specialist roles, which is reflected in our Workplace Gender Equality Agency (WGEA) median gender pay gap of 26.8% and average gender pay gap of 21.3% as at 31 March 2025. We will continue to work to reduce our gender pay gap across the Bank and continue taking action to accelerate progress.

## **Fostering employee wellbeing** 

We are committed to providing a positive and supportive environment where our people feel safe and respected at work. Our protect, care and thrive model helps guide our decision making. 

## **Care**

We care for our people’s health and wellbeing and want them to feel comfortable bringing their whole selves to work. 

We want to protect our people, customers and communities by reviewing and improving our programs to mitigate risk and minimise harm. 

We want to optimise our people’s potential, performance and prosperity. 

To learn and get better every day, we actively engage with our teams to understand how to improve the wellbeing experience and deliver the right tools, learning resources and assistance to help our people reach their full potential. 

Our people-centred approach is informed by data, driving early detection of potential issues, risk profiling that delivers predictive and preventative insights, and evidence-based solutions. We conduct regular health, safety and wellbeing assessments across our domestic and international offices, including targeted reviews in high-risk environments to improve safety maturity. 

Leadership is key to supporting our people in feeling physically and psychologically safe at work. Leadership capability is a key focus and leaders are encouraged to engage in wellbeing conversations, including how to refer team members to appropriate support. 

The Financial Fitness program aims to equip our people with the knowledge and practical steps needed to take control of their finances and pave the way for a brighter financial future. Through a series of engaging and interactive exercises, the program helps strengthen savings habits and supports our people in setting and achieving meaningful financial goals. 

A dedicated early intervention team supports our people with physical and mental health, and wellbeing-related issues. We have enhanced the support provided to our people experiencing difficult situations such as DFV or financial distress. This year we have also launched a GP and telehealth service, providing all our people with easy, direct access to healthcare services at no cost. 

All employees are required to complete mandatory training which reinforces their responsibilities in maintaining a safe, respectful and inclusive workplace. The training covers hazard identification, duty of care and how to escalate concerns appropriately.
