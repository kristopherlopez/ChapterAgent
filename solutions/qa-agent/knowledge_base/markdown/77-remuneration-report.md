## **Overseeing executive performance and remuneration outcomes** 

One of the Board’s responsibilities is to establish and assess executive performance and remuneration targets and outcomes. 

When determining annual performance scorecards, the Board considers past performance, internal forecasts, and external expectations, to set targets that are stretching and aligned to strategic areas of focus. 

The Board Committees meet concurrently to review executive performance against shareholder, customer, people and leadership, and strategic outcomes. These meetings include consideration of audit outcomes and risk assessments to embed a strong risk culture, and in June also consider remuneration outcomes so they are balanced and reflective of overall performance, with opportunity to adjust remuneration for material risk and reputational matters, as appropriate. 

The Board considered a number of matters in the 2025 financial year which for the ELT resulted in two downward and one upward adjustments for risk and reputation matters. 

## **Remuneration decisions** 

In determining executive remuneration, the Board considered financial and non-financial performance outcomes in the context of the 2025 financial year and over a multi-year period. 

To better align our pay against the ASX (including peer banks) and offshore comparator groups, fixed remuneration (FR) increases ranged from 4.5% to 16.7%. The CEO’s FR was adjusted by 14% which is the first increase received since the 2022 financial year. This increase reflects the CEO’s demonstrated strong performance, capability, and the size and scale of the Bank relative to peers.

The Board continues to monitor year-on-year progress to address the gender pay gap. We are committed to reducing our gender pay gap through recruitment, development, retention, greater access to flexible work arrangements, challenging gender role stereotypes, and encouraging and supporting more women to pursue careers in technology and other specialist roles.
