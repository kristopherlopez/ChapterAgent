# Likely Builds — PetSure Australia Chapter Area Lead

What the team (chapter of data scientists across Australia and India) would likely build.

---

## Traditional ML (Risk Management)

- **Credit risk models** — PD, LGD, EAD models for loan decisioning
- **Fraud detection models** — real-time transaction scoring
- **Collections models** — propensity to pay, optimal contact strategy
- **Regulatory capital models** — APRA-aligned risk-weighted asset calculations
- **Feature Store pipelines** — shared feature engineering for training-serving consistency

## GenAI / LLM Systems

- **RAG-based knowledge systems** — policy lookup, regulatory Q&A for risk analysts
- **Agentic workflows** — automating model validation tasks, report generation, compliance checks
- **LLM-as-a-Judge evaluation pipelines** — automated quality scoring for GenAI outputs
- **Prompt engineering frameworks** — versioned, tested prompt pipelines with approval gates

## Platform & Reusability (Chapter Area Lead's direct focus)

This is where the JD says "AI Reusability Assets, Innovation and Frameworks":

- **Unified AI portal** — single pane of glass for all risk management AI tools, insights, and guidance
- **Monitoring templates** — deploy-with-monitoring-built-in for every model (drift, performance, fairness, alerting)
- **Compliance-as-code frameworks** — APRA model risk governance baked into deployment pipelines
- **Standardised architecture / reusable components** — shared libraries, model templates, CI/CD patterns
- **Agent governance framework** — audit trails, permission policies, cost controls, MCP hooks
- **Central dashboards** — model health, GenAI quality, cost attribution, regulatory reporting

## Key Distinction

Chapter members embedded in crews build the **models**. The Chapter Area Lead and any platform-focused members build the **system that makes all of it consistent, visible, and governable**.

The JD's emphasis on "reusability" and "unified portal" suggests PetSure Australia wants to move from one-off model builds to a scalable, repeatable platform — which is essentially the PetSure story (lifting capability at scale) applied to a much larger org.

The most differentiating builds would be the **unified portal** and **compliance-as-code** — those are the ones PetSure Australia is specifically calling out as strategic priorities, not just business-as-usual model building.
