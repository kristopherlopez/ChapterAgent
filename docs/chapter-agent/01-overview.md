## What This Is

The product is the **chapter platform itself** — the reusable infrastructure layer that every AI solution in Risk Management would pass through. The demo shows it working, not in theory, but with real solutions running through real governance.

### The Platform (what the chapter builds)

- **Compliance-as-code pipeline** — automated deployment gates, real-time monitoring, evidence export
- **Evaluation harness** — golden dataset testing, quality thresholds by risk tier, cross-solution scoring
- **Guardrail framework** — scope containment, PII detection, faithfulness checks, bias/toxicity scanning
- **Observability layer** — step-level tracing, cost tracking, audit trail
- **Unified portal** — solution registry, compliance health dashboard, portfolio view, evidence export

### Demo Solutions (what squads would build, passed through the platform)

Each solution maps directly to a responsibility in the JD:

| Demo Solution | JD Responsibility | What It Demonstrates |
|---|---|---|
| **RAG Policy Q&A** — answers questions about risk management policies from a document corpus | Pioneer advancements in AI-powered risk management | Context retrieval, citations, guardrails, evaluation harness in action |
| **Agentic Model Validation Workflow** — LLM agent that reads model documentation, runs validation checks, drafts findings | Provide standardised architecture, reusable AI solution components | Agentic architecture, tool use, compliance gates on an autonomous workflow |
| **Multi-Platform Agent** — same agent implemented across 6 frameworks (LangChain, LangGraph, CrewAI, Semantic Kernel, OpenAI Agents SDK, Claude Agent SDK) | Champion responsible adoption of AI / framework evaluation | Framework-agnostic reusable components, comparative evaluation scorecard |

### The Portal (how it all comes together)

The unified portal shows the portfolio view — all demo solutions registered, their compliance health, evaluation scores, and evidence packages. This maps directly to JD item #2: "Develop and implement a unified portal encompassing all AI-driven risk management tools, insights, and guidance."

An auditor opens the portal, sees three solutions. Clicks into one, sees compliance health. Clicks "Export Evidence," gets a structured report. No meeting. No form. That's the demo.

The JD calls for:
1. Pioneer advancements in AI-powered risk management

Alex explicitly called out that there is heavy manual effort to usher AI solutions through governance processes — teams are adhering to policies manually. This is the exact problem compliance-as-code solves, and the agent should demonstrate this directly. Every demo solution passes through automated governance checks as a live example of what "compliance as a byproduct, not a burden" looks like.
