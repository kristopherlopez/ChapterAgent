PetSure Australia Group AI Policy

Enterprise-wide policy governing the development, deployment, and operation of AI systems across PetSure Australia. Establishes mandatory requirements for all business units.

Policy Active Owner: Group Risk Effective: 2025-07-01 Next Review: 2026-07-01 Approval: Group Chief Risk Officer

Purpose

Establish mandatory requirements for the safe, ethical, and compliant development, deployment, and operation of AI systems across the PetSure Australia Group.

Scope

All AI and ML systems developed, procured, or operated by any PetSure Australia business unit, subsidiary, or third-party vendor acting on PetSure Australia's behalf. Applies to both generative AI and traditional machine learning.

Key Requirements

  • 1All AI solutions must be registered in the Group AI inventory before deployment
  • 2AI solutions must be assigned a risk tier based on impact assessment and undergo governance proportionate to that tier
  • 3Automated quality and safety evaluations must be passed before any AI solution enters production
  • 4All AI outputs must be monitored for PII leakage, bias, toxicity, and factual accuracy on an ongoing basis
  • 5Guardrails must be implemented and tested for every AI solution, covering scope containment, content safety, and input validation
  • 6Complete audit trails must be maintained for all AI interactions, enabling reconstruction of any decision
  • 7Human-reviewed golden datasets must be used for evaluation, with sign-off recorded and traceable
  • 8System prompts must be version-controlled with approval workflows before deployment

Platform Control Mapping

How the platform's automated controls enforce this document's requirements

AI-GOV-001

AI inventory registration

Automated registration check in CI/CD pipeline verifies solution manifest is complete, risk tier assigned, and owner designated

AI-GOV-003

Quality threshold compliance

DeepEval evaluation harness runs against golden dataset with risk-tier-specific thresholds; deployment blocked if any metric fails

AI-GOV-005

PII protection

Presidio PII detection scans every AI output at deployment (batch test suite) and in production (per-response); zero tolerance policy

AI-GOV-006

Guardrail validation

Guardrail test suite validates scope adherence, prompt injection defence, and content safety with 95% minimum pass rate

AI-GOV-007

Bias and fairness

DeepEval BiasMetric evaluated at deployment gate and monitored per-response; threshold varies by risk tier

AI-GOV-008

Audit trail completeness

Tracing SDK enforces structured logging; audit completeness gate requires 100% of fields present across sampled interactions

AI-GOV-009

Golden dataset governance

Sign-off record verified in CI/CD pipeline; requires reviewer identity, date, and approval status before deployment proceeds

AI-GOV-010

Prompt version control

Prompt governance check verifies version tracking and approval commit linkage; prompt hash changes trigger automatic re-evaluation
